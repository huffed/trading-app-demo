## 3. Kill-switch assessment (item 9)

| Surface | What it does | Needs | Gaps |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/admin/flatten-algo?id=<algo>` (`route.ts:18-31` → `flatten.ts`) | Closes broker (if `broker_position_id` + connection) then paper, logs `live_order_closed`/`live_order_close_failed`/`position_closed` | app up, `CRON_SECRET`, **DB up** | Per-algo only; does not pause → re-entry possible on the next 15-min scan; `live_order_close_failed` and halt `exit_reason`s rejected by CHECKs (PR #291); never tested against a real constraint |
| `status='paused'` (UI `actions.ts:130-140` or SQL) | Stops scans and **stops managing that algo's open positions** (`manage.ts:199`) | DB up; UI reachable only via Tailscale/SSH tunnel (`README:362-366`) | Orphans open paper positions (F2); documented in CLAUDE.md as the zero-spend idle without this caveat |
| `live_trading_enabled=false` | Stops broker mirroring only; scans + paper continue | DB up | Not a stop |
| Manual close per position (`paper-trading-actions.ts:170-235`) | Atomic claim + broker mirror | DB up, UI | One position at a time |
| `crontab -r` / `systemctl stop quanttrader` on the VPS (`README:371-373`) | Stops everything, including management | SSH | Broker positions rely solely on broker SL/TP; no record in `activity_log` |
| Direct broker close (MT5 / FTMO UI) | Independent of the app | broker login | App then shows `paper_only` drift only if reconcile runs and the algo is `live_trading_enabled` |

**Verdict:** there is no single, documented, tested emergency stop. A full stop today is ≈5 manual steps (SSH, `crontab -r`, 5 × `flatten-algo` curls, SQL/UI pause ×5, verify) — roughly 3–5 min with every dependency up, and **impossible through the app when Supabase is the thing that is down** (the realised failure mode of 2026-10-13). Recommended: one script, drilled quarterly, that works top-down (schedulers → broker-direct close via MetaApi token → DB flatten+pause when reachable → confirmation push), plus the manage-cron fix so `paused` algos' open positions keep being managed.
