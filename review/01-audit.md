## 1. Look-ahead
- **daily_bias (the July fix).** Live is leak-free: it uses `alignCompletedDailyIndex` (strictly-prior UTC date) on start-stamped OANDA D1 with the forming session dropped at fetch. The backtest is **staler, not leaky** (A6): the close-stamped sessions plus the date-prefix rule select the session that closed a day earlier on the 17:00/21:00 bars. Direction of the bias on the baseline: conservative by ~5% of signals; direction on live-vs-backtest parity: live trades more, slightly worse.
- **Indicators on unclosed bars.** None found on the deterministic path: pattern detectors use bars ≤ i; ATR14 and the 200-bar percentile use closed bars; `computeSlDistance` uses bars ≤ i; OANDA `complete` candles only. The stagnant-exit MFE includes the signal bar's high in the backtest but not live (`portfolio-backtest.ts:1047` vs `engine-position-mgmt.ts:226-245`) — minor, and stagnant exits never fired on the 14 trades.
- **Signal fill timing.** Backtest: signal bar close + 0.5 bps. Live: quote at the first scan after close, or hours later (A10). Neither uses the next bar's open.
- **Archived LLM harness**: forming-session leak + absolute prices/dates in the prompt (A8).

## 8. Overfitting, selection and sensitivity
- Variant count for Phase 2.4: documented lower bound ≈ 2,000, plausible ≈ 3,300 (repo ledger 3,276), effective independent trials ≈ 100–300 (near-collinear families). OOS window redefined ≥ 10 times; the deployed set had **zero holdout**; the 2025-06-18 cutoff was chosen because it maximised passers (`validate-algo.ts:421` calls it "Data-snooped").
- Sensitivity (A15): smooth, flat clusters at ±1 step for lookback, RR, ATR buffer and SMA period; the degenerate lookback 1 collapses (−0.20R); the regime/ADX filters add little mean R; `daily_bias` off adds trades and P&L for every algo. Full tables in `review/results/param-sensitivity.json`.
- **Power arithmetic (A1).** sd(R) = mean_r / sharpe_r from `g8-baseline.json` → 1.36–1.60 per algo ($10K); portfolio sd 1.52 ($10K) and 1.77 ($100K). SE(30) = 1.77/√30 = 0.32. Band half-width 0.077 (old) or 0.103 (new). P(|Z| < 0.103/0.32 = 0.32) ≈ 25%. Trades for 80% power, one-sided 95%, H1 mean R = baseline: n = ((1.645+0.842)·sd/μ)² = (2.487×1.77/0.343)² ≈ 165; with the old baseline (1.52/0.255) ≈ 220. At 4.2 trades/month (live since 2026-07-20) that is 39–52 months; at the backtest's 6.1/month, 27–36 months.
