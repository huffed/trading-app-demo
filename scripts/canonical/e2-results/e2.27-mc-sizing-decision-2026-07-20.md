**Instrument:** `mcChallenge` (`lib/pinned-eval.ts`) — 10,000 synthetic run-until-target challenges per arm, built by **21-trading-day block resampling** of the historical daily path (realized-return deltas + open-position floating-MAE levels, de-compounded). Blocks preserve loss-clustering + concurrency; iid shuffling would understate tails. Verified by `verify-mc-challenge.ts` (9 known-answer assertions incl. censoring monotonicity + seed reproducibility).
