## Methodology-level result: E2.7 PASS

The pre-registered E2.7 PASS criterion was "at least 1 of N=4 candidates passes cluster-stability ≥6/10". **Grid ARB achieves 10/10 cluster-stability** — the methodology refinement is empirically validated. The cluster-stability semantic correctly identifies that grid ARB's peak REGION is robust under bar resampling, even though no specific point variant survives.

**Why cluster-stability works on Grid ARB but fails on the other 3:**

- Grid ARB has 96 variants with a relatively wide top-region distribution — the original top-3 spreads across geometry space such that resampling typically preserves at least 1 of the original variants in the new top-3.
- Grid Engulfing has 96 variants but tighter top clustering at `rr25/rr3/rr5 + lb6 + r1 + rf0_af1` — the top region is narrower so resampling more often replaces all 3 with siblings.
- BO ARB (40 variants) converged to a 4-5 variant cluster around `rr3.0 lb12 r0.45 rf1 af0` — under resampling, ANY of those siblings can win → original top-3 displaced.
- BO Engulfing (40 variants) converged to the high-rr=5.0 edge of the search space — extremely tight cluster, original top-3 cannot survive resampling.

**Empirical lesson:** cluster-stability is the right semantic for WIDE-grid surfaces; tight-cluster (BO-converged) surfaces need an even-lower MIN_INTERSECT or a different test entirely (e.g., "does the resampled top-3 stay within Sharpe ε of the original top-3?"). The pre-registered MIN_INTERSECT=1 is correctly the most-lenient possible; further loosening would be data-snooped.

## Ship-gate result: E2.7 FAIL (no aggregate-≥3/4)

No candidate hits F2 aggregate ≥3/4 even with cluster-stability flipping F2.3 for grid ARB. Grid ARB lifts from 1/4 → 2/4 but the F2.1 + F2.2 failures still dominate. Per the pre-registered ordering, this triggers E2.8.

## Critical discovery: F2.2 is the universal bottleneck (4/4 FAIL)

Across ALL 4 candidates, F2.2 pattern-leave-N-out fails:
- Grid Engulfing cell (XAU/USD, 4h, Long, Engulfing): 2 of 13 non-survivor patterns pass per-candidate (need ≥3)
- Grid ARB cell (XAU/USD, 4h, Long, AsianRangeBreak): 0 of 16 non-survivor patterns pass (need ≥3)
- BO ARB inherits Grid ARB's cell verdict (geometry-independent): 0 of 16
- BO Engulfing inherits Grid Engulfing's cell verdict: 2 of 13

**This is the deeper finding:** at every gold-only 4h cell tested, NO OTHER patterns produce independent per-candidate-passing signal. The "survivor pattern" is unique — interpretable as either (a) genuine pattern-specific signal (good) or (b) multiple-testing artifact from enumerating 14 patterns × the lucky-pattern wins (bad). F2.2's strict gate ≥3 was designed to distinguish (a) from (b); empirically (b) is more consistent with the data.

**Why this matters for E2.8 + E2.9:**

E2.8 pre-registered recalibration relaxes the deflation gate (PBO <0.5 → <0.6) which would unlock BO ARB's deflation gate (PBO 0.557 → PASS). BUT BO ARB's F2 aggregate is still 1/4 — F2.1 + F2.2 + F2.3 all fail. PBO recalibration alone doesn't unlock ship.

For ANY candidate to reach F2 aggregate ≥3/4, F2.2 must change. The leave-N-out test is structural at the universe level (pattern × cell), not the candidate level — relaxing F2.2 thresholds risks data-snooping (we'd be calibrating to fit the current data's pattern-uniqueness, which may not generalize).

## Filed in roadmap: E2.7.5 — Cell-Coverage Test (the missing lever)

The F2.2 universal failure suggests a NEW lever that's NOT in the current E2.6/E2.7/E2.8/E2.9 path: re-running F2.2 on the AUGMENTED universe (H.4b feature-augmented patterns), not the unaugmented universe. If feature-augmented patterns produce ≥3 passers at a cell, the F2.2 verdict CAN flip without relaxing thresholds. This tests the genuine cell-coverage hypothesis empirically.

Filed as new roadmap item **E2.7.5** (between E2.7 and E2.8) — see ROADMAP.md for full discovery + composition + pre-registered gate.
