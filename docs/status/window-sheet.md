# The window: the sheet

It replaces `2026-11-21-window-sheet.md`, which stays as the record of the 21 and 22 November plan. It holds for a window on either day and on any weekend.

For the operator and for the session started in `../qt-window`. The plan is `docs/PHASE_D_PLAN.md`, section "The next window", on `dev`; this sheet only points at it. Nothing merges into `dev` from Friday 20:00 UK until step 2 has merged #342 (operator, 2026-11-25), or, if #342 is not on the list, step 1 does not pass or a Sunday deadline below is missed, until the window is over or moved. Nothing starts before Friday 22:00 UK; whatever is unfinished by Sunday 18:00 UK waits a week, except a step already started (below). Every host command prompts, and the session runs one only when you ask.

The window is one block, on Saturday or on Sunday (below: **the day**). The operator confirms the day by Friday 18:00 UK; if neither, it moves a week and nothing else changes. A Sunday window leaves out 2d and 2e, which wait for the next window that runs on a Saturday. On a Sunday, the latest starts: step 1 passed by 13:30 UK; #342 merged by 14:00 (step 2 ends by about 15:45); the deploy by 15:30 and the reboot by 17:10, with or without #342; step 3 by 15:45; step 4 by 17:25; step 5 by 17:45; step 6 by 17:55; step 7 by 17:15. They hold for any step run on the window's Sunday, a Saturday window's too. Missing 13:30, or #342 not merged by 14:00, takes #342 off READY TO MERGE until the next window and holds steps 2, 3 and 5 for a week; if #342 merged late all the same, step 3 runs by 15:45 and the deploy, the reboot and step 5 wait. Missing 15:45 holds step 3 alone, which then runs first in the next window; a missed deploy or reboot start holds that step; step 4 or 5 that misses its time waits a week (step 6 then follows step 4); step 6 or 7 that misses its time is dropped. A step already started is finished that evening, or put back by its own rollback, before 22:00 UK, and past that you decide; a deploy's rollback cannot start from 21:00 UK. A Saturday window may overrun into the Sunday until 18:00, but `verify-full` and the paused-pairs install start only on the Saturday. A Saturday window reads step 6 on the Sunday, by 12:00 UK, and needs you then, for the read and any rollback; a read after 12:00 is still made.

Three values are filled in after the last merge before the hold, and nowhere else:
- `<dev head>`: what `git fetch origin && git rev-parse --short=12 origin/dev` prints after the last merge before the hold.
- `<#342 head>`: what `gh pr view 342 --json headRefOid --jq '.headRefOid[0:7]'` prints after its last rebase, as listed in READY TO MERGE.
- `<expected list>`: what the plan's step 2 second command prints with `COMMIT` set to `<dev head>` (the plan says what each file means; here each file gets a reason, all workstation tools or CI).
